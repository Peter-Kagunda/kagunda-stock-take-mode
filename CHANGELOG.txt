==================================================
Changelog: Stock Take Mode for WooCommerce
==================================================

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

--------------------------------------------------
[1.0.1] - 2026-09-21
--------------------------------------------------
Display name
- The plugin's display name is now "Stock Take Mode for WooCommerce".
  The plugin slug and text domain (kagunda-stock-take-mode-for-woocommerce)
  are unchanged.

New
- Status card at the top of the settings screen with one-click "Start now"
  and "Stop now", plus a "Turn off" link in the admin toolbar.
- "Preview as customer" button (shows the restricted store to an
  administrator without logging out).
- Banner color presets, live banner preview, banner position (sticky top,
  top, bottom) and scope (all pages, or shop pages only).
- Quick "ends in 1 hour / 4 hours / 1 day / 3 days" scheduling buttons.
- Waitlist search, per-subscriber removal, "Clear list" and "Email everyone
  now" tools.
- Waitlist privacy note under the email field.
- WordPress personal data export/erase support and suggested privacy policy
  text for waitlist emails.
- Countdown now uses the server clock and reloads the page shortly after it
  reaches zero.
- {shop_url} placeholder for the re-open email.
- Developer hooks: kagstm_is_window_active, kagstm_message,
  kagstm_show_banner, kagstm_capability, kagstm_broadcast_batch_size,
  kagstm_waitlist_max, kagstm_waitlist_rate_limit, kagstm_started,
  kagstm_ended, kagstm_waitlist_subscribed.

Improved
- Re-open emails are queued and sent in small background batches.
- Start/end notifications are scheduled with WP-Cron so they fire on time
  even when nobody is visiting the store.
- Purchasing is blocked in the Cart and Checkout blocks (Store API) and on
  classic checkout submissions, in addition to the existing protections.
- Accessibility of the banner and waitlist form.
- Modern, responsive settings screen; tab choice is remembered.
- Uses WooCommerce's bundled selectWoo instead of a bundled select2 copy.
- Settings are registered with typed defaults and validated in one place;
  imports use the same validation and are limited to 256 KB.
- Plugin code split into focused classes under /includes.

Fixed
- An administrator (or other bypass role) browsing the store during a stock
  take could mark it as "ended" and trigger the waitlist broadcast early.
- Admin bar indicator and dashboard widget showed the wrong status for
  administrators.
- In "hide add-to-cart buttons" mode, excepted products lost their button.
- Waitlist sign-ups are now rate limited, honeypot protected, size capped
  and no longer autoloaded.
- CSV export neutralises spreadsheet formulas.
- Uninstall removes transients and scheduled events as well as options.
- Playground blueprint now configures the demo store correctly.

--------------------------------------------------
[1.0.0] - 2026-09-13
--------------------------------------------------
- Initial public release.
- Core purchasing restriction engine with scheduled start/end windows.
- Product and product-category exception lists.
- Role-based bypass for store staff.
- Live countdown timer banner for frontend notifications.
- Waitlist lead capture form and CSV export.
- Automatic re-opening email broadcast to waitlist subscribers.
- Admin bar status indicator and dashboard analytics widget.
- Settings import/export as JSON.
- WooCommerce log integration (WooCommerce > Status > Logs).

--------------------------------------------------
Roadmap
--------------------------------------------------
- Recurring stock-take windows.
- Per-product "keep purchasable during stock take" checkbox.

--------------------------------------------------
How to update
--------------------------------------------------
Update from the WordPress dashboard (Dashboard > Updates or Plugins). Your
settings are preserved.
